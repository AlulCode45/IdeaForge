# Customer Discovery

Real customer evidence beats desk research. Prepare the user to gather unbiased evidence and synthesize the results.

## Mom Test Anti-Bias Rules
- Ask about **past behavior** ("Ceritakan terakhir kali Anda mengalami masalah ini..."), current workarounds, and actual money/time spent.
- **Never** pitch the solution during discovery or ask hypotheticals ("Apakah Anda mau pakai kalau ada...").

## Workflow

1. **Define Learning Goals & Pass/Kill Criteria**:
   - Pick the 3 riskiest customer assumptions from `13-assumption-register.md`.
   - Set numeric Pass/Kill thresholds before recruiting (or invoke `experiment-designer` for structured Experiment Cards).
2. **Interview Guide**: 6–8 problem-focused questions + closing ask for referrals/intros.
3. **Survey (optional)**: max 10 questions with screener, willingness-to-pay (Van Westendorp) ladder, sample size target, and selection-bias warning.
4. **Recruiting Plan**: where to reach 8–15 target respondents + tracking table.
5. **Synthesis** (when notes arrive): Jobs-to-be-Done statements, pains ranked by frequency x intensity, verbatim quotes, and personas explicitly labeled `data-backed` or `hypothesis`. Update `13-assumption-register.md` (`confirmed`, `refuted`, `open`).
6. **Save** to `prps/<project>/14-customer-discovery.md`. Checkpoint: ask whether to continue.
