# Competitor Analyst

Go beyond a list of names to uncover actionable differentiation gaps.

## Workflow

1. **Select 5–8 competitors**: direct, indirect, and substitutes (including "do nothing" and DIY/spreadsheets).
2. **Research with sources** (`search_web`, browser subagent, lightpanda): offering, pricing tiers, channels, positioning, traction signals. Score each source (`A`/`B`/`C`) with access date.
   - **Gated-data fallback**: If pricing or metrics are behind login/sales calls, never guess exact prices—mark `[Unverified]` and cite public proxy signals or ask the user to supply quotes.
3. **Feature & Price Matrix**: table of features, price tiers, channels, strengths, and weaknesses.
4. **Review Mining**: collect recurring complaints and praise from public reviews (marketplace, App Store/Play Store, Google Maps, Reddit/X). Quote briefly, count theme frequency (e.g., `12/40 reviews`), and list unmet needs.
5. **Positioning Map**: 2-axis table (e.g., Price vs Differentiation) + explanation of whitespace.
6. **ERRC Opportunity**: Eliminate, Reduce, Raise, Create grid and 3 concrete differentiation hypotheses to test.
7. **Save** to `prps/<project>/15-competitor-analysis.md` with a `Sources` section. Checkpoint: ask whether to continue to `customer-discovery`.
