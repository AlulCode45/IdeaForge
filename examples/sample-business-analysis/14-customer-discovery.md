# Customer Discovery & Experiment Plan (Sample)

## 1. Riskiest Customer Assumptions

1. `A1`: Workshop owners are willing to pay IDR 199,000/month after experiencing time savings on commission math and service reminders [Assumption].
2. `A2`: Monthly churn can be kept <= 4.5%/month via a 30-minute onboarding session [Assumption].
3. `A3`: Field + local-ad CAC stays <= IDR 280,000 per paying workshop [Assumption].

## 2. Experiment Card: 15-Workshop Concierge Pilot (14 Days)

- **Method**: Visit 30 workshops in Malang/Surabaya; offer free spare-part catalog setup + 14-day free trial.
- **Pass Criteria**: >= 10 active workshops logging at least 5 transactions/day for 10 days, and >= 4 converting to paid IDR 199,000/mo.
- **Kill Criteria**: < 2 workshops willing to pay after 14 days -> revisit value metric or pricing.
