# SWOT & TOWS Matrix - BengkelKu SaaS (Sample)

| Dimension | Key Points |
|---|---|
| **Strengths** | Vertical focus (mechanic commissions + license-plate history), 86.4% SaaS gross margin, 1.6-month CAC payback (`11`, `15`) [Estimate] |
| **Weaknesses** | Zero initial brand awareness; requires hands-on onboarding during early rollout (`07`, `14`) [Assumption] |
| **Opportunities** | 30,000+ urban workshops not yet using vertical software; spare-part distributor integration in Phase 2 (`07`) [Estimate] |
| **Threats** | High churn if owners skip inventory input; horizontal POS players adding basic workshop fields (`15`) [Estimate] |

## TOWS Strategies
- **SO**: Leverage pre-loaded part templates to accelerate pilot conversion in dense workshop clusters.
- **WO**: Offer free initial setup of the top 100 spare parts during Week 1 to reduce early churn.
- **ST**: Lock in retention through customer service-history records and automated WhatsApp reminders (high switching cost).
- **WT**: Do not scale national ad spend until the first 30-day cohort proves monthly churn <= 4.5%.
